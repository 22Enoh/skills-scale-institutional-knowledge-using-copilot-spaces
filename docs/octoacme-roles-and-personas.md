# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Managers

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers oversee execution across multiple workstreams and ensure delivery remains aligned to schedule, scope, and quality expectations. They coordinate with project managers, engineering leads, and stakeholders when delivery risks or dependencies need escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Track delivery milestones across multiple teams or workstreams
- Resolve delivery blockers and escalate risks when needed
- Align sprint or iteration progress with project timelines
- Coordinate handoffs between planning, execution, and release activities
- Support status reporting for leadership and stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** to keep delivery plans current and realistic
- Partner with **Product Managers** to align scope decisions with business priorities
- Coordinate with **Developers** to remove blockers and manage sequencing
- Communicate with stakeholders when timeline or scope changes affect expectations

---

## Program Coordinators

### Role Summary
Program Coordinators support the administrative and operational flow of project management work. They help keep documentation, scheduling, and follow-up actions organized so teams can focus on execution.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain meeting schedules, agendas, and action items
- Track follow-ups, notes, and open decisions across workstreams
- Keep process documentation current and easy to find
- Assist with reporting and coordination across multiple projects
- Support logistics for reviews, checkpoints, and governance meetings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Support **Project Managers** by organizing schedules, notes, and follow-ups
- Work with **Delivery Managers** to track milestones and action items
- Coordinate with **Product Managers** and **Developers** to capture decisions and dependencies
- Help ensure stakeholders receive timely updates and meeting outcomes

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and ensure deployment activities happen in a controlled and visible way. They help connect development completion to production or downstream delivery steps.

### Responsibilities
- Define release readiness checklists and go/no-go criteria
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Confirm dependencies, approvals, and rollback plans are documented
- Ensure release communications are shared with the right stakeholders
- Track issues discovered during release preparation or deployment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Developers** to confirm changes are complete and ready for release
- Partner with **Project Managers** to align release timing with delivery plans
- Coordinate with **Product Managers** on release scope and customer impact
- Inform stakeholders about deployment timing, risks, and release outcomes

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons act as the communication bridge between project teams and external stakeholders. They help ensure expectations, feedback, and updates flow clearly between groups.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder questions, concerns, and feedback
- Translate project updates into stakeholder-friendly communication
- Share decisions, progress, and risks with impacted audiences
- Coordinate review cycles or approvals when stakeholder input is needed
- Help reduce communication gaps between teams and business partners

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** to communicate status and changes
- Partner with **Product Managers** to share roadmap and scope updates
- Coordinate with **Delivery Managers** when delivery risks affect stakeholder expectations
- Support **Release Managers** by communicating release timing or impacts

---

## Risk Owners

### Role Summary
Risk Owners are accountable for specific risks or issue areas and help track mitigation actions to completion. They ensure that project risks are visible, understood, and actively managed.

### Responsibilities
- Own assigned risks and monitor their status over time
- Define mitigation and contingency actions for known risks
- Escalate when risk exposure becomes unacceptable
- Keep risk registers updated with current information
- Confirm follow-up actions are completed or reassessed

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** to keep the risk log current
- Partner with **Delivery Managers** when risks affect timelines or dependencies
- Coordinate with **Developers** on technical risks and mitigation work
- Inform **Product Managers** and stakeholders when risks affect scope or outcomes

---

## Documentation Stewards

### Role Summary
Documentation Stewards help maintain the quality, clarity, and consistency of project documentation. They ensure important process information stays accurate and usable as work evolves.

### Responsibilities
- Review and update process docs when roles, workflows, or decisions change
- Keep documentation clear, consistent, and easy to navigate
- Track outdated content and identify missing guidance
- Help standardize templates, terminology, and structure across docs
- Support knowledge sharing for new team members and stakeholders

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** to keep project process docs current
- Partner with **Product Managers** and **Delivery Managers** when workflows or responsibilities change
- Coordinate with **Developers** and **Release Managers** to capture process updates from implementation and release work
- Help teams document decisions and lessons learned for future reference

---
